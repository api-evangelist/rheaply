When organizations introduce or optimize internal asset reuse, they don’t often consider how product variance impacts their efforts. Overlooking variance can quietly derail timelines and slow progress towards goals. In a recent inventory engagement led by Rheaply’s field services team, 1,400 items were cataloged across 615 distinct products — a 44% product variance.
